music-pill

A small web component that shows the song on your site and links to it on Spotify. One script, one tag, any framework. Add audio you're licensed to play and it fades in, loops, and fades out.

The pill in the corner of this page is the real component.

Add it to a page

No build step

<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/music-pill@0.1.0/dist/auto.js"></script>

<music-pill
  spotify-url="https://open.spotify.com/track/5GjUhwxykUVYCYkiNo4BEM"
  song-title="Strobe"
  artist="deadmau5"
></music-pill>

With a bundler

npm install music-pill

import 'music-pill/auto';

React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, Astro and Angular use the same element. Setup notes for each are in the README.

Two ways to use it

Link only

Artwork, title and artist, linking to the track on Spotify. No audio, no sound button, nothing that suggests music is playing.

With your own audio

Point audio-src at a file of the same recording that you're licensed to play on a website. Browsers usually block sound until a visitor interacts, so the pill adds a small sound button.

A Spotify link doesn't grant any right to play the song, or any other audio, on your site. The component never downloads or plays audio from Spotify.
